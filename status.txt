## MAINTENANCE CONFIG

### Model

MaintenanceConfig

Stores:

* Building
* Calculation Type
* Fixed Amount
* Rate Per Sqft
* Effective From
* Active Status

Calculation Types:

* fixed
* per_sqft

### Responsibility

Maintenance rule define karna.

Example:

₹10 per sqft

or

₹5000 fixed

History maintain hoti hai.

Rate change hone par naya config create hota hai.

---

## MAINTENANCE BILL

### Model

MaintenanceBill

Stores:

* Flat
* Bill Type
* Month
* Year
* Amount
* Paid Amount
* Pending Amount
* Due Date
* Status
* Notes

Bill Types:

* opening
* monthly
* special

Statuses:

* unpaid
* partial
* paid

### Responsibility

Accounting ledger.

Har maintenance charge yahi store hota hai.

---

## PAYMENT

### Model

Payment

Stores:

* Flat
* Amount
* Unallocated Amount
* Receipt Number
* Payment Mode
* Transaction Id
* Remarks
* Paid At

Payment Modes:

* online
* cash
* cheque

### Responsibility

Payment transaction ledger.

Advance amount bhi yahi track hoti hai.

Example:

Paid = ₹50000

Outstanding = ₹10000

Remaining ₹40000

Stored in:

unallocated_amount

---

## PAYMENT ADJUSTMENT

### Model

PaymentAdjustment

Stores:

* Payment
* Bill
* Adjusted Amount

### Responsibility

Audit Trail.

Example:

Payment = ₹10000

Allocated:

Bill 1 = ₹3000

Bill 2 = ₹5000

Bill 3 = ₹2000

Sab mapping yahan store hogi.

========================================================
SERVICE LAYER
=============

Business Logic Layer

---

## BillGenerationService

Responsibilities:

* Monthly Bill Generation
* Opening Due Creation
* Special Charge Creation
* Bulk Bill Generation
* Duplicate Prevention
* Config Selection by Effective Date

Rules:

occupied → bill

vacant → bill

unsold → no bill

---

## PaymentAllocationService

Responsibilities:

* Receive Payment
* Allocate Oldest Dues First
* Create Payment Adjustments
* Update Bill Status
* Store Advance Balance
* Partial Payment Handling
* Full Payment Handling

Example:

Outstanding = ₹10000

Payment = ₹50000

Allocation = ₹10000

Advance = ₹40000

---

## ReceiptService

Responsibilities:

* Receipt Data Generation
* Payment Summary
* Bill Mapping
* Adjustment Summary
* PDF Ready Data
